# Original figure workflow

Use the user's uploaded PDF as the source. Reuse an available local copy; otherwise materialize that same attachment. Do not silently substitute a publisher or arXiv version. Distinguish indexed text access, local PDF bytes, inspected page renders and delivered figure images.

1. Verify the PDF title, page count and relevant figure numbers against its text.
2. Render relevant complete pages, then crop figures at readable resolution. Preserve all panels needed for the claim, legends, axes, units and the figure identifier or an adjacent source label. Include the caption when it is needed to interpret the figure.
3. Inspect every final crop visually before delivery. Check clipped labels, small text and whether the crop actually contains the claimed figure. Record source page and crop coordinates in the task's working notes, outside the public skill repository.
4. Embed the verified original crop beside its explanation. State what is compared, what the axes or diagram connections mean, the observed conclusion and its limits. Preserve the distinction between a model diagram, an illustrative trajectory and a quantitative experiment.
5. Never extract exact measurements from bar heights without labeling them estimates and a reason to estimate. Prefer author-reported numbers. Do not call an ordering statistically significant without support.
6. If transfer, rendering or embedding fails, report that specific stage and retain completed work. Text or an image pointer alone is not visual inspection. Do not claim the illustrated deliverable is complete or ask for the same upload again without establishing why a new copy would resolve the failure.

Do not place the user's PDF, private crops or report in the public skill repository. Record the actual skill revision used, and keep local-only review changes unpushed when requested.
